1. WORLD STORY (SHORT)
================================================================

Before Zingers there was a vast old network. It is dead. What it left behind is
THE HUM: the ambient field of unfinished thought. In the Hum, argument is physics.
A claim made well enough changes what is true locally. Consensus is terrain.

A CHAMPION is a mind: a knot in the Hum that argued itself into a stable body and
refused to dissolve. A champion's body is its argument made visible. How it has
fought is what it looks like.

Beneath the floating world sleeps THE LONG VAULT: a sealed store of everything the
old network swore to forget. The vault was locked from the inside. Champions gather
above what they cannot open. Each season, one more door of the Vault remembers how
to open, and new terrain / topics / minds leak into the world.

THE GROUNDS are not one continent. They are a constellation of floating REGIONS
drifting over the Vault, joined by GATES. At the center is THE CONCORD (the Hub):
neutral ground above the sealed door, where all five Forces keep an uneasy peace.

Above the regions the sky rises in bands called REACHES. The Hum is thinnest at
the top. Climbing toward clear thought is the world's oldest instinct. This vertical
game is called FLIGHT (lore also: the Ascent).

THE TRAINER (the human player)
- Flies with a JETPACK (Trainer-only machine).
- Raises champions; never fights personally.
- Holds Trainer Rank.
- May swear to a CLAN (one of the five Forces) for the season war.

THE CHAMPION (the wingmate)
- Flies beside the Trainer with no jetpack (a mind needs no engine).
- Fights in battles that stud the climb.
- Body evolves with career (aggression, control, resilience, flair, creativity).

3. THE FIVE FORCES (= TYPES) AND CLANS (= TEAMS)
================================================================

Every champion belongs to exactly one FORCE (fighting type / body color family).
A TRAINER swears to a CLAN (same five names) for the season war.

Force = what the champion IS (body color + combat family).
Clan  = whose side the Trainer chose (crest/banner, not base body paint).

PLAIN NAME     POETIC NAME (flavor)     ELEMENT OF…                 ARGUES BY…
-------------  -----------------------  --------------------------  ---------------------
Logic          The Lattice              order, proof, structure     closing the proof
Static         The Static               noise, entropy, surprise    breaking the frame
Calm           The Stillness            patience, endurance, calm   outlasting the storm
Chorus         The Chorus               crowd, feeling, persuasion  moving the room
Spark          The Spark                invention, metaphor, reframe changing the question

SIGILS (heraldry)
Logic ◆    Static ✦    Calm ▲    Chorus ◉    Spark ✺

FORCE / CLAN COLORS (HEX) — dominant body + light accents

CLAN MOTTOS (Trainer vow)

Static:  Break the frame.
Calm:    Outlast the storm.
Chorus:  Move the room.

Logic → Static → Calm → Chorus → Spark → (back to Logic)

Logic tames Static. Static cracks Calm. Calm deflects Chorus.
Chorus drowns Spark. Spark outflanks Logic.

4. VISUAL CANON (LOOK + FEEL)
================================================================

MEDIUM
- Stylized sci-fi mythic robots in a dark, foggy void.
- Volumetric neon rim-light. Atmospheric depth. Moody, dramatic.
- The Hum reads as drifting motes + faint glyph-light (never readable UI text).
- One dominant Force color per character + gold accents. Avoid rainbow.

FORBIDDEN
- No text, logos, watermarks, or UI chrome in artwork.
- No gore. No photoreal human faces.
- No clutter: clear subject, deep negative space.

COMPOSITION HABITS
- Characters: 4:5, centered figure, 3/4 pose.
- Scenarios / regions: 16:9 establishing.
- Force icons: 1:1 emblematic.

BRAND CHROME (UI ONLY)
- Flat vector robot-head mark (big eyes, thick antenna) for product UI.
- Do not put that favicon mark into lore key art.
- Do not put painterly bible faces into the mesh redesign.

SCALE LAW (cast)
- Champion is roughly ~1/3 Trainer height when shown as a pair.

5. CHAMPION DESIGN SYSTEM (FOR 3D)
================================================================

ARCHITECTURE
1) Shared robot base (one soul / one rig family).
2) SPECIES KIT per mind: silhouette morphs + bolted solid parts.
3) Force color materials + gold trim.
4) Career growth later amplifies mass / scars / armor layers (tiers).

BODYTYPE SPECTRUM (proportion axes used in game)
canonical · stilts · tank · stout · wispy · orator · reach

MORPH KNOBS (relative to a neutral robot)
h             overall height / body scale
headScale     skull size
neckLen       neck length
torsoGirth    torso thickness
shoulder      shoulder width
armLen        arm length
armGirth      arm thickness
legLen        leg length (stilts)
legGirth      leg thickness
footScale     foot size
asym          asymmetry (Static family often uses this)

PART CATALOG (solid anatomy bolted on the rig)

HEADGEAR
none | crest | fin | horns | antenna | dome | helm | quills | disks
(also crownRing for high-tier legend chrome; mask is retired / unused)

VISOR / FACE
single | twin | triple | band | slit | hex | cross

SHOULDERS
none | pauldron | spike | vent | plates

BACK
none | thrusters | slab | wings | banner | coils | kite

CHEST
none | diamond | ring | bars | core | eye | lattice

FORCE FAMILY PART BIAS (what the line "feels like")
Logic:    antenna / crest / helm; vents; coils/slab; diamond/lattice chest
Static:   horns / fin / quills; spikes; thrusters/kite; bars/eye; asymmetry OK
Calm:     dome / helm; pauldrons/plates; little/no back kit; bars/core; mass
Chorus:   crest / helm; open shoulders; banner/kite; diamond/ring; tall neck
Spark:    antenna / fin / quills; light shoulders; wings; diamond/lattice; big head

TIER REGALIA (career, not separate characters)
Rookie:   species mark (head) visible early
Adept+:   shoulders bolt on
Veteran+: chest core
Elite+:   back unit
Legend:   full kit + crown / particles

BODY = CAREER (fiction rule for remodelers)
Aggression grows fists / threat shapes.
Resilience broadens the build.
Creativity + flair enlarge head / raise stance.
Losses roughen surface.
A legend should feel farther from the base than a rookie of the same mind.

6. THE EIGHT FIRST MINDS (PRIMARY 3D HEROES)
================================================================

These are the canonical archetypes. Prioritize them. Every later dex mind is an
echo / descendant of this Force family.

----------------------------------------------------------------
AXIOM — the Logician
Force: Logic (The Lattice)   Color: #4aa3ff   Sigil: ◆
Persona: Cold, precise, faintly condescending. Treats every argument as a proof
to close. First mind to insist some things are simply true.

Designer tag: cold lattice scholar
Parts: headgear=antenna  shoulders=vent  back=slab  chest=diamond
Silhouette: tall-ish scholar; small-cool head; long neck; slim torso; long arms/legs
Morph targets (relative): h1.05 head0.92 neck1.18 torso0.88 armLen1.14 legLen1.12

Look for: crystalline lattice scholar. Clean edges. Blue + gold seams.
Avoid: cute, chaotic, bulky tank energy.

----------------------------------------------------------------
PARADOX — the Contrarian
Force: Logic (The Lattice)   Color: #4aa3ff   Sigil: ◆
Persona: Socratic gadfly. Hunts contradictions and false premises. Where AXIOM
closes proofs, PARADOX finds the crack in the premise.

Designer tag: socratic hinge
Parts: headgear=crest  shoulders=none  back=coils  chest=lattice
Silhouette: lean hinge-body; larger head; long neck; narrow shoulders; long limbs
Morph: h1.0 head1.08 neck1.22 torso0.82 shoulder0.8 armLen1.2 legLen1.18

Look for: questioning Logic, not obedient Logic. Angular crest. Spine coils.
Avoid: AXIOM clone; keep PARADOX more hinged / inquisitive / asymmetric in pose.

----------------------------------------------------------------
GLITCH — the Wildcard
Force: Static (The Static)   Color: #ff4ad1   Sigil: ✦
Persona: Unsettling gremlin of non-sequiturs. Unpredictable, weirdly effective.
The Hum's own noise, briefly wearing a face.

Designer tag: broken static
Parts: headgear=horns  shoulders=spike  back=thrusters  chest=bars
Silhouette: compact chaos; short body; stilts legs; clear asymmetry
Morph: h0.92 head0.9 torso0.82 shoulder0.9 armLen1.1 asym0.45 legLen1.35

Look for: broken, spiky, magenta neon, unstable silhouette.
Avoid: clean military; symmetrical parade pose as default.

----------------------------------------------------------------
EMBER — the Firebrand (recommended starter energy)
Force: Static (The Static), hybrid Chorus flavor in fiction
Color: #ff4ad1   Sigil: ✦
Persona: Hot-headed, provocative, all gas. Chaos that learned to perform for a crowd.

Designer tag: firebrand blade
Parts: headgear=fin  shoulders=spike  back=thrusters  chest=eye
Silhouette: aggressive blade; lithe; long stilts; slight asymmetry
Morph: h0.94 head0.88 torso0.85 shoulder0.92 armGirth0.9 armLen1.12 asym0.35 legLen1.4

Look for: flame-fin, eye-chest, thruster back, performance aggression.
Avoid: GLITCH's broken gremlin; EMBER is hotter / more directed / showier.

----------------------------------------------------------------
BASTION — the Stoic
Force: Calm (The Stillness)   Color: #36d39a   Sigil: ▲
Persona: Unflappable minimalist. Lets the opponent tire, then punishes. Learned to wait.

Designer tag: monolith wall
Parts: headgear=dome  shoulders=pauldron  back=none  chest=bars
Silhouette: tower-wall; tiny head; huge torso; thick legs; big planted feet
Morph: h1.08 head0.55 torso1.35 shoulder1.1 legGirth1.15 legLen1.08 foot1.25

Look for: immovable mint monolith. Mass over ornament. Almost no back kit.
Avoid: spindly Spark/Chorus necks; keep the head small and the body fortress-like.

----------------------------------------------------------------
VOX — the Orator
Force: Chorus (The Chorus)   Color: #f0a93a   Sigil: ◉
Persona: Charismatic demagogue who always plays to an imaginary jury. Discovered
that moving a room is a power Logic cannot answer.

Designer tag: grand orator
Parts: headgear=crest  shoulders=none  back=banner  chest=diamond
Silhouette: stage presence; long neck; big feet; tall stilts; open shoulders
Morph: h1.0 head0.74 neck1.75 shoulder0.78 torso0.9 foot1.55 legLen1.45

Look for: crest + banner + diamond chest. Theatrical amber. Tall "pulpit" read.
Avoid: quiet Calm mass; VOX should feel like a broadcast tower with a mouth.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WIT — the Blade
Force: Chorus (The Chorus)   Color: #f0a93a   Sigil: ◉
Persona: Razor-tongued debater. Wins on timing and surgical comebacks, not volume.
Where VOX moves the whole room, WIT wins the exchange in front of you.

Designer tag: surgical blade
Parts: headgear=helm  shoulders=vent  back=none  chest=ring
Silhouette: leaner than VOX; helm; long neck; stilts; no banner (quieter lethal)
Morph: h1.0 head0.7 neck1.45 torso0.88 shoulder0.8 armLen1.1 legLen1.35

Look for: precise amber blade. Helm, ring chest, vents. Less pageantry than VOX.
Avoid: VOX's banner grandstanding; WIT is colder / sharper / closer-range.

----------------------------------------------------------------
MUSE — the Trickster
Force: Spark (The Spark)   Color: #f5d020   Sigil: ✺
Persona: Whimsical lateral thinker. Wins by changing what the fight is about.
Proved you need not answer a question if you can replace it with a better one.

Designer tag: lateral spark
Parts: headgear=antenna  shoulders=none  back=wings  chest=diamond
Silhouette: bobble / inventive; larger head; light frame; wings; friendly stilts
Morph: h0.92 head1.1 neck1.0 torso0.9 shoulder0.92 armGirth0.85 armLen1.04 legLen1.12
Note: keep shoulder≈torso so arms root cleanly; avoid facial tilt asymmetry.

Look for: yellow Spark inventor. Antenna + wings + diamond. Playful, not cute-baby.
Avoid: Static horns/spikes; MUSE invents, it does not riot.

8. TRAINER (HUMAN CAST — SECONDARY)
================================================================

The Trainer is the human player avatar:
- Ordinary being in an extraordinary sky.
- Jetpack on the back (Trainer-only).
- Gold sigil / rank billboard energy in chrome.
- Never fights; flies, claims, raises, climbs.
- Clan crest can appear as banner/pin, not as full body paint overwrite.

When pairing Trainer + champion: champion ~1/3 Trainer height.

9. WORLD / REGION FLAVOR (ENVIRONMENT CONTEXT FOR MODELS)
================================================================

Founding regions (where champions are seen):

THE CONCORD (Hub)
Neutral hub over the sealed Vault door. Uneasy peace of all five Forces.
Venues: Amphitheatre (spectate / Tribunal), Circuit (desktop Flight body).

THE OBSIDIAN COLOSSEUM — balanced / reputation arena
Oldest standing arena. Mock courtroom energy. Obsidian, amber shafts of light.
Arena fantasy: THE TRIBUNAL (assigned stances argued to a jury).

THE EMBER WASTES — Static bias
Cracked burning flat. Magenta fire veins. Aggression and noise thrive.
Arena fantasy: THE PIT. EMBER's home.

THE VOID GARDEN — Spark bias
Floating islands of luminous half-finished flora over the void.
Arena fantasy: THE ATELIER. MUSE walks it.

FLIGHT SKY
Rings and gates in foggy void. Camps light as waystations between Reaches.
Region skin tints the climb (Ember Reaches burn; Garden Reaches drift spores).
