# 06 · The Chronicle: living, generative seasons

> **In short:** The game runs in seasons, like a TV show. Each season the game
> auto-generates a new chapter of story, fresh debate topics, and a featured tilt
> on one of the three founding regions. Your champions and board name carry over.
> The whole running story is called the **Chronicle**.

The **Chronicle**. The world's ongoing, season-by-season story. Advances one step
at a time: a **season** is the Vault remembering one more seal. It is the game's
content cadence and its story engine at once. Seasons are **generative and seeded**:
from a single season seed plus this bible, the game derives the season's story,
topic bank, region tilt, and featured echo, reproducibly, and never off-canon.

The live map stays the **three founding regions**. Seasons tilt which one is
featured; they do not ship a fourth landmass today.

## What a season turns over

| Element | What changes each season | Source |
|---------|--------------------------|--------|
| **The Arc** | A short narrative: which seal stirred, what leaked out | generated from seed + canon |
| **Topic bank** | The season's propositions (the "forbidden questions" the door remembered) | generated, themed to the door |
| **Region tilt** | A featured founding region + its force-bias | chosen for force balance |
| **Featured echo** | A generated name in a First Mind's line. Season flavor, not a new recruitable card | generated lineage |

## Champion sagas (personal generative narrative)

Beyond the world story, **each champion accrues its own saga**. Its personal,
evolving life-story. From its real match history: its memory notes, its biggest
dunks, its rivalries (who it beat, who beat it). The generator turns this into a
short, evolving in-character biography that
lives on the champion's card and profile. The record is already there; the season
engine just narrates it.

## Generator contract (canon discipline)

The generative layer must:
1. Take a **seed** (the season number is the default seed) so output is reproducible.
2. Read this bible as canon and **never rename** forces, regions, or First Minds.
3. Produce **additive** lore only: a new door, not a retcon.
4. Degrade gracefully: with no model available, fall back to a **deterministic**
   season composed from canon tables (so the game always has a live season).

The optional model pass enriches the Arc and topic flavour on top of that
deterministic backbone.
